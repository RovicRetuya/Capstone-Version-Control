COMMON QUESTIONS

Is DeFaketive free to use?

Yes. Guests get 3 new scans a day, and a free account gives unlimited scans.

How does DeFaketive decide on a warning?

It checks reviews for safety, fake and defect reports separately. A warning appears when enough reviewers report the same problem, and a single safety report is always shown. Every warning quotes the reviews behind it.

Which platforms are supported?

Product links from Shopee PH, Lazada PH and Temu PH.

Can it detect Taglish reviews?

Yes. The sentiment model was trained on Filipino and English reviews, and the warning word lists include Taglish terms like peke, sira and umusok.

What happens if reviewers report a problem?

You'll see a warning with the reviewers' own words highlighted. Read them, then compare similar products with no warning signs before you buy.

How accurate is the check?

It is not perfect. In our tests against reviews checked by people, it missed about one in five real safety reports, and about half of what it flagged was harmless. That is why every report quotes the reviews themselves. Use it as decision support, not proof.

Shopping safety tips

Check seller ratings first
Prefer sellers with high ratings and many completed orders before buying electronics.
Verify product authenticity codes
Many brands print a serial or authenticity code you can check on their official website.
Read 1-star reviews carefully
Counterfeit products often appear in low-rating reviews mentioning 'fake', 'not original', or unexpected materials.
Check the return policy
Prefer sellers with a clear return policy for electronics, and keep the receipt and box.

About DeFaketive

A safer way to read what shoppers say before you buy.

What it does

DeFaketive reads customer reviews from Shopee, Lazada and Temu PH (in English, Filipino or Taglish) and shows what buyers report about fakes, defects and safety problems, quoting their own words.

Its warnings summarize what reviewers wrote. They are not proof that a product is fake, defective or unsafe, so decide for yourself before buying.

Terms & Privacy

Terms of Use

DeFaketive is a student research project for academic use. Its warnings summarize what reviewers wrote; they are not proof that a product is fake, defective or unsafe. Decide for yourself before buying.

Please don't misuse the service, for example by automating scans.

Privacy Policy

With an account, we keep your name, your email and a scrambled copy of your password. Your password itself is never stored. We also keep your scan history and wishlist so they follow you to any browser.

For guests, we count scans per day using a browser cookie.

We don't sell or share any of this. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account, or email the team at 23ur0560@psu.edu.ph.
